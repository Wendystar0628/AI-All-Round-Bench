03Whitebox Rendering

Glass Greenhouse and Florist's Workshop

From bare geometry to golden hour

A whitebox with only shapes and cameras, no color: a Victorian glass greenhouse joined to a florist's workshop. Materials, lighting and color grading are added in Blender to render a summer dusk, as stills and a 60-second fly-through.

  1. i.Low sun through the glass dome, falling in slanted shafts
  2. ii.Amber and pale-cyan panes casting colored light on the floor
  3. iii.Lanterns, candles and stove fire glowing in the dim corners
  4. iv.A verdigris-mottled fountain, a gleaming glass cloche
