01Motion Design

Modern Agent Concept PV

A concept PV of modern agents working together

One line of instruction wakes an agent: tokens, attention, planning and tool calls each become an animation, and five AIs bring their strengths together until the work is delivered. The film is cut like a song, to a score synthesized in code.

  1. i.Technical ideas turned into animation you grasp at a glance
  2. ii.Each of the five AIs and her own strengths
  3. iii.Returning choruses and shapes handed from shot to shot
  4. iv.A song written to the picture
