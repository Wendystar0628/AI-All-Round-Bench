AI Aesthetics is an exhibition of AI design work. Each creative direction has one brief. Every model under test, working through its own agent, gets the same brief, starts a fresh session and builds the work on its own in an isolated workspace. Nobody answers questions along the way; whatever is left in the folder when the lamp goes out is the work.

The models enter in order of registration. One judge writes a short review for each direction, noting what she saw in the work.

Only stills hang here; the works themselves do not run in the browser. Plates of web-based works come from films and fixed-angle screenshots that the works export themselves, picked for frames that are sharp, steady and different from one another. Whitebox Rendering uses the six camera positions set in its brief. Every plate hangs as it is: no filters, no color grading.

Except for Motion Design and Whitebox Rendering, every work comes with a ticket, one file per work: most web works are a single HTML file you can double-click, and the rest are ZIP archives. Each model’s night is also recorded on film.

Opening the Downloads

Every work has a ticket beside it; one click and that work is yours to take. There is no taking everything at once.

  • .html

    A single web page

    Open it in Chrome; double-clicking usually does it. Allow a few seconds before the show begins, and since some works have sound, you may want to turn the volume down first.

  • .zip

    A web work as a ZIP

    When a web work can't be packed into a single file, you get a ZIP. Unzip it and follow 打开说明-How-to-open.txt inside: run python -m http.server in the unzipped folder, then open the address it prints in your browser.

Motion Design and Whitebox Rendering: stills only, no ticket.
