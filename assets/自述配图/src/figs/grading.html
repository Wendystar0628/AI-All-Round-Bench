From Time-Up to Score

从到时到成绩From time-up to score

一个型号答完一个打分专题以后的流程；评分员、评分指南和权重由各专题定。What happens after a model finishes a scored topic; graders, guides and weights are set by each topic.

01

到时收卷Collect

结束作业对象，作答目录原样留下；会话日志移出受测账户

The Job ends, the work folder stays as it is, and session logs leave the tested account

02

排队等机器Queue

还有需要安静机器或独占整机的作答在跑，评分就排队等着

Grading queues while any quiet-machine or whole-machine answering is running

03

主体评分Main grading

每个评分任务一个新会话，按专题评分指南评；能算的由程序算；最多 16 个同时

A fresh session per job, by the topic's guide; anything computable is computed; up to 16 at once

04

性能评分Speed grading

有性能项的题，等主体全部评完再测，一次一个，独占机器

Tasks with a speed part are measured after all main grading, one at a time, alone on the machine

05

合成总分Combine

按专题清单的评法和权重，把各题分数合成型号总分

Task scores are combined into the model's total by the topic manifest's method and weights

06

发布与归档Publish

各题正式结果齐备、确认后上网站；提交物、评分表和开销归档，逐文件记 SHA256

Online once every task has a confirmed result; submissions, score sheets and costs archived with a SHA256 per file
